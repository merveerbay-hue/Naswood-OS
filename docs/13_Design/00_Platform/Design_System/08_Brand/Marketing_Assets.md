**Module:** Design System

**Category:** Brand

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

# Purpose

The Marketing Assets standard defines the design principles, branding rules and asset specifications for all marketing materials related to Naswood.

The objective is to ensure a consistent corporate identity across digital, printed and event-based marketing materials.

All marketing assets must comply with the official Naswood Brand Guidelines.

---

# Objectives

- Consistent Brand Identity
- Professional Marketing Materials
- Cross-Platform Consistency
- Print and Digital Readiness
- Accessibility
- Scalable Asset Library

---

# Design Principles

Marketing assets should be

- Professional

- Clean

- Premium

- Industrial

- Modern

- Consistent

Every asset should reinforce the Naswood brand.

# Do

✓ Use official logo

✓ Use corporate colors

✓ Maintain typography

✓ Optimize images

# Don't

✗ Stretch logos

✗ Use unofficial fonts

✗ Mix color palettes

✗ Use low-resolution images

✗ Ignore brand approval

# Acceptance Criteria

Marketing assets follow the official brand guidelines.

Assets are optimized for their target medium.

Version control is maintained.

Approval workflow is completed.
