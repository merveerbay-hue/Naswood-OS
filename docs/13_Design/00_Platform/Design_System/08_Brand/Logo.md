# Logo

**Module:** Design System

**Category:** Brand

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

---

# Purpose

The Logo standard defines the official usage, placement and presentation rules for the Naswood brand identity across all digital and printed products.

The logo is the primary visual identifier of Naswood and must be used consistently throughout the platform.

---

# Objectives

- Maintain Brand Recognition
- Ensure Consistent Usage
- Support Accessibility
- Standardize Digital Assets
- Protect Brand Integrity

---

# Design Principles

The Naswood logo should always be

- Clear
- Recognizable
- Consistent
- High Quality
- Undistorted

The logo should never compete with business content.

# Do

✓ Preserve aspect ratio

✓ Use official assets

✓ Maintain minimum size

✓ Respect clear space

---

# Don't

✗ Stretch the logo

✗ Change brand colors

✗ Add effects

✗ Overlay on busy images

✗ Modify typography

# Acceptance Criteria

Official logo assets are used.

Minimum size requirements are met.

Clear space is maintained.

Correct variant is selected.

Corporate colors are preserved.

Accessibility requirements are satisfied.

Logo renders correctly across all supported platforms.
