# Photography

**Module:** Design System

**Category:** Brand

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

---

# Purpose

The Photography standard defines the visual style, quality requirements and usage guidelines for all photographic assets used throughout Naswood OS.

Photography should reinforce the Naswood brand by showcasing authentic manufacturing, wood products, craftsmanship and industrial excellence.

All photographic assets must follow the official photography standards.

---

# Objectives

- Consistent Visual Identity
- Authentic Industrial Representation
- Professional Image Quality
- Brand Recognition
- Accessibility
- Cross-Platform Consistency

---

# Design Principles

Photography should be

- Authentic

- Professional

- Natural

- Industrial

- Minimal

- High Quality

Real production environments should always be preferred over staged or artificial scenes.
