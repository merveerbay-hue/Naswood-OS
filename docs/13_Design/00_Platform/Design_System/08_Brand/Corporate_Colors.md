# Corporate Colors

**Module:** Design System

**Category:** Brand

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

---

# Purpose

The Corporate Colors define the official brand color palette of Naswood OS.

These colors establish a consistent visual identity across digital products, printed materials, presentations, websites, dashboards, documents and marketing assets.

All interfaces must use the official corporate palette.

---

# Objectives

- Maintain Brand Consistency
- Strengthen Brand Recognition
- Standardize Visual Identity
- Support Accessibility
- Enable Cross-Platform Consistency
- Ensure Scalable Design
