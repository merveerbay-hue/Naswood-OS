# OBJECTIVE

Implement the Maintenance Reports module responsible for generating operational,
analytical and management reports across all maintenance activities.

Maintenance Reports provide historical analysis, operational reporting,
regulatory compliance and executive KPIs using CQRS Read Models.

Reports are read-only.

Reports never modify transactional data.
