# OBJECTIVE

Implement the Quality Reports module responsible for generating operational,
analytical and regulatory quality reports across the Quality Management System.

Quality Reports provide historical, real-time and compliance reporting using
CQRS read models.

Reports are read-only.

They never modify transactional data.
