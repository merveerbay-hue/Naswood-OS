---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the functional testing approach for Naswood OS.

Functional testing verifies that business processes and system features operate according to defined requirements.

---

# 2. Objectives

- Validate business functions
- Verify module behavior
- Ensure process integrity
- Detect functional defects
- Support release readiness

---

# 3. Testing Scope

- Business Modules
- User Workflows
- Business Rules
- Permissions
- Notifications
- Reports

---

# 4. Functional Areas

- Manufacturing
- Inventory
- Quality
- Maintenance
- Sales
- Purchasing
- Logistics
- Finance
- Administration

---

# 5. Test Workflow

Prepare

↓

Execute

↓

Validate

↓

Report

↓

Retest

---

# 6. Success Criteria

- Expected results achieved
- Business rules validated
- No critical functional defects
- User acceptance completed

---

# 7. Principles

- Requirement-based testing
- End-to-end process validation
- Repeatable execution
- Documented results
- Traceable defects
