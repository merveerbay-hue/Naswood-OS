**Project:** Naswood OS

**Document:** Digital Twin Architecture

**Code:** DT-001

**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Digital Twin architecture of Naswood OS.

The Digital Twin provides a real-time virtual representation of the factory by synchronizing physical assets, manufacturing processes, inventory and business operations.

---

# 2. Objectives

- Create a digital representation of the factory
- Synchronize physical and digital assets
- Enable real-time monitoring
- Support simulation and analysis
- Improve operational visibility
- Support AI-driven decision making
