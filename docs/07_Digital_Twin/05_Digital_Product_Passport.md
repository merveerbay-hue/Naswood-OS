# Digital Product Passport

**Project:** Naswood OS

**Document:** Digital Product Passport

**Code:** DT-005

**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Digital Product Passport (DPP) framework of Naswood OS.

The DPP provides a digital identity for every product, enabling complete traceability from raw material to customer delivery.

---

# 2. Objectives

- Establish product identity
- Ensure end-to-end traceability
- Support sustainability
- Improve customer transparency
- Enable lifecycle tracking

---

# 3. Passport Contents

- Product Information
- Material Information
- Batch Information
- Production History
- Quality Records
- Certifications
- Sustainability Data
- Shipment Information

---

# 4. Product Lifecycle

Raw Material

↓

Production

↓

Quality

↓

Warehouse

↓

Shipment

↓

Customer

---

# 5. Traceability

The passport shall link:

- Product
- Batch
- Production Order
- Machine
- Quality Records
- Shipment
- Customer

---

# 6. Access

The Digital Product Passport may be accessed through:

- QR Code
- Barcode
- RFID
- Customer Portal
- Factory Copilot
