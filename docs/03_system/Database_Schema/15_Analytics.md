# Database Schema — Analytics

**Project:** Naswood OS
**Document:** Analytics Schema
**Database:** PostgreSQL
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved

---

# Purpose

The Analytics module provides operational intelligence for Naswood OS.

It transforms manufacturing data into measurable performance indicators, dashboards, trends, forecasts and AI-ready datasets.

Analytics never modifies operational data.

It consumes data from operational modules and produces insights.

---

# Philosophy

Operations generate data.

Analytics transforms data into information.

Artificial Intelligence transforms information into recommendations.

Analytics is read-only.

# Business Rules

### BR-1501

Analytics shall never modify operational data.

---

### BR-1502

KPIs shall be calculated automatically.

---

### BR-1503

Historical KPI values are immutable.

---

### BR-1504

Forecasts shall be stored separately from actual values.

---

### BR-1505

Dashboard configuration is user-specific.

---

### BR-1506

Reports shall be reproducible.

---

### BR-1507

Snapshots are read-only.

---

### BR-1508

Analytics calculations shall be traceable to source data.

---

### BR-1509

AI-generated forecasts shall include confidence levels.

---

### BR-1510

Historical analytical data shall never be deleted.
