# Database Schema — Artificial Intelligence

**Project:** Naswood OS
**Document:** AI Schema
**Database:** PostgreSQL
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved

---

# Purpose

The Artificial Intelligence module provides intelligent assistance, predictive analytics, optimization and decision support throughout Naswood OS.

AI never replaces business logic.

AI analyzes manufacturing data, generates recommendations and supports users in making better decisions.

Final decisions remain under human or Workflow control.

---

# Philosophy

Manufacturing generates data.

Analytics creates insights.

Artificial Intelligence creates recommendations.

Users approve actions.

AI never modifies operational data directly.

# Business Rules

### BR-1901

AI shall never modify operational data directly.

---

### BR-1902

All AI recommendations require human or Workflow approval before execution.

---

### BR-1903

Every AI execution shall be logged.

---

### BR-1904

Model versions shall be traceable.

---

### BR-1905

Recommendations are immutable.

Status changes create new records.

---

### BR-1906

AI predictions shall include confidence values.

---

### BR-1907

Users may provide feedback to improve recommendation quality.

---

### BR-1908

Knowledge sources are version-controlled.

---

### BR-1909

AI-generated recommendations shall create Business Events.

---

### BR-1910

AI interactions affecting business decisions shall generate Audit Logs.

# AI Philosophy

Artificial Intelligence is an advisory layer within Naswood OS.

AI enhances human decision-making by analyzing manufacturing data, identifying patterns and generating recommendations.

Operational authority always remains with approved business processes and authorized users.

The objective of AI is to increase productivity, quality, efficiency and operational insight while preserving safety, traceability and governance.
