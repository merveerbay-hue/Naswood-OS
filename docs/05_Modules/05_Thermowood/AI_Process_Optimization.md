# 1. Purpose

The AI Process Optimization module serves as the intelligent optimization engine of Naswood OS.

It continuously analyzes production, quality, energy, maintenance, genealogy and operational data to improve productivity, reduce costs, increase quality and support autonomous decision-making.

The module acts as the AI Factory Brain of the Thermowood Manufacturing Execution System (TMES).
