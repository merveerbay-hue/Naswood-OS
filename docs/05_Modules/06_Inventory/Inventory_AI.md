# 1. Purpose

The Inventory AI module provides intelligent inventory optimization, warehouse analytics and autonomous stock management across the entire Naswood OS platform.

It continuously analyzes inventory movements, warehouse capacity, production demand, purchasing plans and logistics activities to optimize inventory levels, storage efficiency and material availability.

The module serves as the Inventory Intelligence & Autonomous Warehouse Platform (IIAWP) of Naswood OS.

---

# 2. Objectives

- Optimize inventory levels
- Reduce excess inventory
- Prevent stock shortages
- Improve warehouse efficiency
- Optimize material flow
- Support AI-assisted inventory decisions
- Synchronize Digital Twin
