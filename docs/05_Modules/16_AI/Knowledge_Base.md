# Knowledge Base Module

**Project:** Naswood OS

**Document:** Enterprise Knowledge Base

**Module Code:** MOD-AI-KB-001

**Version:** 2.0

**Status:** Enterprise

---

# 1. Purpose

The Knowledge Base module provides the centralized enterprise knowledge platform for Naswood OS.

It stores, indexes, classifies and retrieves structured and unstructured knowledge used by AI Agents, Factory Copilot, Digital Twin and enterprise applications.

The module serves as the Enterprise Knowledge Intelligence Platform (EKIP) of Naswood OS.

---

# 2. Objectives

- Centralize enterprise knowledge
- Preserve corporate know-how
- Improve AI reasoning
- Support semantic search
- Enable Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Synchronize enterprise knowledge
- Support continuous learning

# 16. Business Rules

All enterprise knowledge shall be version-controlled.

AI responses shall reference source documents whenever possible.

Knowledge retrieval shall respect user permissions.

Knowledge indexing shall occur automatically after approval.

Historical document versions shall remain accessible.

Every document shall have ownership and review dates.
