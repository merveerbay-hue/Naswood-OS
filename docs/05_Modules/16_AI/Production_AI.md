# 1. Purpose

The Production AI module provides intelligent production optimization, predictive manufacturing analytics and autonomous production assistance across all manufacturing operations.

It continuously analyzes production orders, machine performance, material flow, quality results, energy consumption and operational events to optimize production efficiency, product quality and manufacturing costs.

The module serves as the Production Intelligence & Autonomous Optimization Platform (PIAOP) of Naswood OS.

---

# 2. Objectives

- Optimize production performance
- Reduce manufacturing costs
- Improve OEE
- Increase timber recovery
- Minimize scrap and rework
- Improve production planning
- Synchronize Digital Twin

# 17. Business Rules

Production AI shall continuously analyze live production data.

AI recommendations shall never directly modify production parameters without authorization.

Optimization recommendations shall include confidence scores and supporting evidence.

Every AI action shall be traceable and auditable.

Scenario simulations shall remain isolated from production.
