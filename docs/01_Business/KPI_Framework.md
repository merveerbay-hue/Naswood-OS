# Purpose

This document defines the Key Performance Indicator (KPI) framework for Naswood OS.

The KPI framework provides a standardized method to measure operational performance across manufacturing, inventory, quality, maintenance, logistics and commercial activities.

All KPI calculations shall be based on real production data and complete material traceability.

---

# KPI Categories

Naswood OS groups KPIs into the following categories:

- Production
- Material Yield
- Inventory
- Quality
- Maintenance
- Logistics
- Energy
- Cost
- Sales
- Sustainability
- AI Performance

# KPI Rules

- Every KPI must have a documented calculation formula.
- Every KPI shall have an owner.
- Every KPI shall have a target value.
- Every KPI shall support trend analysis.
- KPI values shall be calculated automatically from production data.
- Manual KPI calculations are discouraged.
- KPI history shall never be deleted.
- AI recommendations shall reference KPI impacts where applicable.

---

# KPI Philosophy

Naswood OS measures performance through facts rather than assumptions.

Every KPI shall be derived from traceable manufacturing data.

Reliable decisions require reliable measurements.
