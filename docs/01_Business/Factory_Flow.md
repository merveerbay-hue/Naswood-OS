# Factory Flow

**Project:** Naswood OS

**Document:** Factory Flow

**Code:** BUS-002

**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the end-to-end manufacturing flow of Naswood OS.

It establishes the standard production lifecycle from raw material receiving to customer shipment, ensuring that every module follows the same operational process.

---

# 2. Objectives

- Standardize factory operations
- Define production lifecycle
- Ensure end-to-end traceability
- Synchronize business modules
- Support AI and Digital Twin
- Optimize manufacturing efficiency

---

# 3. Factory Overview

Naswood Factory transforms raw logs into value-added engineered wood products through a controlled manufacturing process.

Every production step is digitally tracked and synchronized across the platform.
