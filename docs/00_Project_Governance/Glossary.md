# Purpose

This document defines the common terminology used throughout Naswood OS.

Every business term, technical concept and manufacturing object shall have one official definition.

All documentation, software, APIs and AI assistants shall use these definitions consistently.

---

# General Terms

## Business Code

A human-readable identifier assigned to a business object.

Example

MAT-PN-000123

Business Codes are displayed to users.

Business Codes are not primary keys.

---

## UUID

A globally unique identifier generated by the system.

UUIDs are immutable.

UUIDs are never shown to end users.

---

## Master Data

Relatively static business information shared across the system.

Examples

Company

Factory

Machine

Material Type

Wood Species

Warehouse

Recipe

---

## Transaction

A business operation that changes the state of the system.

Examples

Receiving

Production

Shipment

Inventory Movement

---

## Event

An immutable historical record describing something that happened.

Events are never modified.

Examples

MaterialReceived

TransformationCompleted

ShipmentCreated

---

# Organization

## Company

The legal business entity.

---

## Factory

A manufacturing facility belonging to a company.

---

## Department

An organizational unit within a factory.

Examples

Production

Quality

Maintenance

Warehouse

Sales

---

## Position

The organizational responsibility assigned to an employee.

Examples

Production Manager

Operator

Planner

---

## Employee

A person working within the organization.

---

## User

A system account linked to an employee.

---

# Manufacturing

## Receiving Lot

The first traceable group of materials entering the factory.

Usually represents one truck or one supplier delivery.

Every material originates from a Receiving Lot.

---

## Material

A uniquely identifiable physical object.

Examples

Log

Prism

Green Lumber

Kiln Dried Lumber

Thermowood Lumber

Lamella

Panel

Profile

Package

Material identity never changes.

---

## Material Type

Defines the classification of a material.

Examples

LOG

PRISM

KD_LUMBER

THERMOWOOD_LUMBER

SOLID_PANEL

PROFILE

---

## Material Attribute

A technical property describing the current state of a material.

Examples

Length

Width

Thickness

Moisture

Density

Thermo Class

---

## Measurement

A recorded observation of a material or process.

Measurements are historical.

They do not replace Material Attributes.

---

## Transformation

A manufacturing process that converts one or more input materials into one or more output materials.

Transformation is the central production object.

---

## Operation

One manufacturing step within a Work Order.

Examples

Sawing

Kiln Drying

Thermowood

Profiling

Finger Joint

Pressing

Packaging

---

## Routing

The sequence of Operations required to manufacture a product.

Routing may vary according to product, species or production strategy.

---

## Recipe

The process parameters required to execute an Operation.

Examples

Kiln Program

Thermowood Program

Press Parameters

---

## Production Batch

A group of materials processed together during one manufacturing execution.

Examples

Kiln Charge

Thermowood Batch

Panel Press Batch

---

## Recovery

A process where reusable material returns to production.

Recovered material remains traceable.

---

## Waste

Material that permanently leaves the manufacturing flow.

Waste must always be classified.

---

# Inventory

## Warehouse

A physical storage area.

---

## Warehouse Location

A specific storage position inside a warehouse.

Examples

Rack

Bin

Floor Area

---

## Inventory Movement

A record describing a change in material location or status.

Inventory is calculated from movements.

---

## Reservation

Allocation of materials to a Production Order or Work Order.

---

# Quality

## Inspection

Evaluation of a material, product or process.

---

## Defect

A non-conformance identified during production or inspection.

---

## Quality Grade

The classification assigned after inspection.

Examples

AA

AB

BC

Reject

---

# Packaging & Logistics

## Package

One or more materials grouped for storage or shipment.

---

## Shipment

A delivery of one or more packages to a customer.

---

# Planning

## Production Order

Defines what needs to be manufactured.

Created from customer demand or stock replenishment.

---

## Work Order

Defines how production will be executed.

Generated from a Production Order.

---

## Production Strategy

Planning method used by the system.

Supported strategies

MTS – Make to Stock

MTO – Make to Order

ATO – Assemble to Order

ETO – Engineer to Order

---

# Maintenance

## Machine

A production asset used to perform manufacturing operations.

---

## Tool

A replaceable production component.

Examples

Saw Blade

Planer Knife

Cutter Head

Router Bit

---

## Preventive Maintenance

Maintenance performed before failure occurs.

---

## Corrective Maintenance

Maintenance performed after failure occurs.

---

A specialized AI service responsible for a defined business capability.

Examples

Planning Agent

Quality Agent

Inventory Agent

Maintenance Agent

---

## Recommendation

A suggestion generated by AI.

Recommendations never execute automatically without authorization.

---

## Prediction

Examples

Yield Prediction

Maintenance Prediction

Delivery Prediction

# Core Principles

Throughout Naswood OS:

- Every physical object has a digital identity.
- Every manufacturing action creates a Transformation.
- Every important action generates an Event.
- Every material remains traceable.
- Recovery is not waste.
- AI supports decisions but does not replace authorized users.
- Business Codes are for people.
- UUIDs are for software.
- The Material is the source of truth for physical production.
