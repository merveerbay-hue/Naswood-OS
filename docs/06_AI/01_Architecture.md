# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Artificial Intelligence architecture of Naswood OS.

The architecture enables AI to operate as a platform-wide capability, supporting every business module through intelligent automation, decision support, prediction, optimization and natural language interaction.

---

# 2. Goals

- Centralized AI architecture
- Modular AI services
- Secure AI operations
- Explainable AI
- Human-in-the-loop decision making
- Real-time manufacturing intelligence
- Continuous learning

---

# 3. AI Principles

- AI assists users, it does not replace them.
- Every AI decision must be explainable.
- Critical actions require approval.
- AI must use enterprise data only.
- Every AI interaction is logged.
- AI models are continuously improved.
