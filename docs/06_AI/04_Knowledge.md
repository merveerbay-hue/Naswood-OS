**Project:** Naswood OS

**Document:** Knowledge Base

**Code:** AI-004

---

# 1. Purpose

The Knowledge Base is the central source of enterprise knowledge for Naswood OS.

It provides trusted, structured and searchable information used by Factory Copilot and AI Agents to generate accurate, explainable and context-aware responses.

---

# 2. Objectives

- Centralize enterprise knowledge
- Ensure a single source of truth
- Support AI reasoning
- Preserve version history
- Enable semantic search
- Improve response quality

---
